Outcome
Generated nine cases: widths N=1,4,16 crossed with canonical, Ready-fact-swap,
and full seeded-shuffle orderings. They contain 6,12,36 records respectively.
All axioms have strength 1/confidence 0.9 and unique singleton evidence IDs.
The query is (CanOperate unit0); its truth value is not supplied as an input.
No costs, route priorities, precomputed answers or artificial delays are supplied
to the engine. Query arguments are 100 steps and 4097 for each queue cap.

Each A or B_i two-step path has an authored static witness. All 72 witnesses
passed independent replay using the pinned ground modus-ponens formula. Every
minimal witness concludes strength 1/confidence 0.729, within the specified
numeric tolerance. A uses evidence [1,3,4]; B_i uses [2,2i+3,2i+4].
The generator computes the unit-strength family's expected confidence using
exact rational powers of 9/10; the separate checker evaluates the general PLN
formula, including its non-unit-strength fallback and evidence guards.

These are checked candidate derivations, NOT captured engine executions.
No native inference ran on these cases. No committed belief-state availability,
search success, first-answer time, cost gap or CPU-budget effect is established.
This is rule-conformance validation, not empirical probability calibration.
The nine cases share one structural family; they are not nine independent domains.
